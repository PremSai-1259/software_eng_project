Digital Customer Onboarding Policy - Example Source

The onboarding service shall verify an applicant's identity before opening an account.
The system must record the identity verification decision, the source used, and the reviewer identifier in an auditable event log.
Personal data collected during onboarding must be restricted to authorised roles and retained according to the approved retention policy.
The onboarding portal should notify applicants when additional documents are required.
The system shall integrate with the approved identity-verification provider through its documented API.
Failed identity-verification checks must be routed to a trained operations reviewer.
The service should provide an availability target approved by the operations owner before production release.
